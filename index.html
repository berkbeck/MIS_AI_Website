Welcome to Our MIS Website

Management Information Systems (MIS) connect people, processes, data, and technology to help organizations make decisions. This website introduces topics that are important to information systems. It explores how artificial intelligence (AI) can support learning. It also explains how malware and hackers threaten everyones digital information. The goal of our website is to encourage responsible and informed technology use.

Management Information Systems help people and organizations collect and organize different data, communication information effectively, and improve daily decisions. Effective information systems can support daily operations and also long term planning. responsible use of artificial intelligence can make learning and work more efficient. Cybersecurity practices help protect important information. The pages below help expand on and explain these ideas and provide links for further learning. Learn more from: Gartner, CISA, and MITRE.
