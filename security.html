Cybersecurity and Malware

What is Malware?

Malware is a type of software that is designed only to hard a computer, disrupt the operations it needs to perform, steal people's personal information, or gain access to a system that wasn't authorized. Malware is a major cybersecurity concern because it affects all people, businesses, schools, and even the government.

Histoical Event: WannaCry

On May 12, 2017, the WannaCry random focused attack spread across many people's computer systems from all over the world. It made certain files encrypted and demanded a ransom, or payment, from users who were affected. This attack disrupted many organizations and even demonstrated the risks of failing to apply security updates that would fight this malware. This incident shows why organizations need to make sure they are updating their software, placing reliable backups, and using strong practices.

Learn more from the Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency .

Types of Malware

  1. Virus:Malicious code that attaches to a file or a program and can spread when it is being used.
  2. Worm:Malware that can copy itself and spread between computers and/or systems.
  3. Trojan Horse:A type of malicious software disguised as a legitimate program.
  4. Randsomware:Malware that strictly restricts access to computer files and demands a payment attached to it.
  5. Spyware:A software that secretly monitors a computer's activity and collects information about it.

Motives for Cyberattacks

  1. Stealing personal, business related, or financial information
  2. Demanding any money through ransom or extortion
  3. Disrupting the flow of business operations or damaging computer systems
  4. Gaining access not authorized to accounts and/or networks

Ways to Prevent Malware and Cyberattacks

  • Install software and security updated as they release
  • Use a strong, unique password and authenticator
  • Avoid clicking on any suspicious links or email attachments
  • Back up important files regularly
  • Keep security updates updated
  • Learn how to recognize malware



Why Cybersecurity Matters to MIS

Management Information Systems help organizations collect, store, share, and process data and information. Protecting their valuable information helps keep systems reliable and make sure business operations can proceed as normal. Malware can expose sensitive information and data, interupt work completely, and create possible financial loss. People and businesses can reduce these risks by using security technology, training employees to recognize malware, and following clear cybersecurity rules and regulations.
